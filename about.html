About me

I'm a Computer Science major at UCF with minors in Secure Computing & Networks and Mathematics, graduating in Spring 2027.

I got into security because I like knowing how things actually work underneath. Most of my coursework and projects have pushed me toward the low level: writing a thread-safe hash table with POSIX threads, building a compiler, simulating a MIPS processor, and reading raw network traffic in Wireshark. That foundation is what I bring to security. It's hard to defend a system, or spot what's wrong with one, if you don't understand how it behaves when everything is going right.

Right now my focus is network security. My senior design project, Cloak, sits squarely in that space: we're building a device that watches a network's traffic and uses machine learning to flag anything that looks malicious, and I work mainly on the AI side. Earning my CompTIA Security+ gave me a broader footing across threats, controls, and incident response. Outside of class I've worked in retail since 2022, where I learned to stay calm under pressure, pick up responsibility past my job title, and work well with a team when things get busy.

Goals

After graduating, I want to start my career in a hands-on security role, ideally in network or defensive security, where I can work with real traffic, real systems, and real incidents. Over time I plan to keep building certifications and deepen my offensive skills so I understand both sides of an attack. Long term, I want to be the person a team trusts to know how a system can fail and how to stop it.
